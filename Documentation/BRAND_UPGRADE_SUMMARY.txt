                        VISUAL HIERARCHY

High Priority:     Navy backgrounds, large headings, white text
Medium Priority:   Orange accents, category names, page numbers
Low Priority:      Cream text, decorative elements, footers

  - Navy demands attention (background)
  - Orange highlights action/page numbers
  - Cream provides elegant secondary information
  - White text ensures readability
